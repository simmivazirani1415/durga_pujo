Use this planning sequence:
Find celebrations open during the chosen window.
Choose one anchor venue based on interests and location.
Reserve time for the chosen activity, such as bhog or a performance.
Add food and an optional second celebration only if the travel budget allows.
Include the trip home, plus a delay buffer.
Explain every recommendation and offer a shorter alternative.
If live traffic is available, use it. Otherwise, label journey times as estimates. Venue opening hours, concert access, bhog availability and Metro operating hours should each be verified independently.
